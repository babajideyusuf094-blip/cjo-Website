Our Programmes

Project Phases

01

Case Lab Project

A biweekly analytical platform where criminological cases are dissected, studied, and used to sharpen participants' skills in justice application and policy thinking.

02

Bail & Restorative Support

Providing structured assistance for bail processes while championing restorative justice frameworks that repair harm, rebuild relationships, and reintegrate offenders.

03

Awareness on Sex Weaponization

An educational initiative tackling the exploitation and weaponization of sex in criminal contexts — raising awareness, sensitizing communities, and equipping individuals.

04

Family Reorientation for Youth

Engaging families as the first layer of crime prevention by reorienting family dynamics, improving communication, and reducing risk factors for youth offending.

05

Juvenile Delinquency Support

A targeted support system for at-risk youth and juvenile offenders — combining counselling, mentorship, legal awareness, and community engagement for positive outcomes.

Leadership

Hamid Alao

Hamid Alao, Criminal Justice Outreach (CJO)

Abdulhamid Adejumo Dhikrullah is an emerging leader and strategist from Osun State with a background in Criminology and Security Studies, an HSE practitioner, and a passionate advocate for leadership development and wellness. His leadership journey reflects a consistent commitment to institutional growth, innovation, and inclusive impact. From championing the inclusion of the first female members of the Students' Representative Assembly (SRA) at the Federal University Dutse, to serving as the pioneer Coordinator of the Junior Chamber International Nigeria (JCIN), Federal University Dutse Local Organization, and contributing to transformational initiatives as the 2024 Auditor General of the National Association of Kwara, Kogi, and Six South-West States' Students (NAKOLES), he has continually demonstrated visionary leadership by building systems, empowering people, and driving meaningful change. As the Convener of the Criminal Justice Outreach (CJO), Abdulhamid led a four-month public awareness campaign focused on often-overlooked drivers of crime and social instability. Through the Case Lab Series, he examined the long-term effects of adverse childhood experiences, social stigmatization of reformed offenders, psychosocial hazards, and family instability. His advocacy promotes a more preventive, rehabilitative, and community-centered criminal justice system, encouraging collective responsibility for strengthening families, fostering social inclusion, reducing recidivism, and advancing sustainable societal development. He is an Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Logistics and Supply Chain Management (AMCILSM), an Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Environmental, Health and Safety (AMCIEHS), an Associate Member of the Electoral College Nigeria (ECN), and a Member of the Institute of Safety Professionals of Nigeria (ISPON). Driven by the conviction that lasting change begins with informed leadership and shared responsibility, Abdulhamid remains committed to advancing safety, security, leadership, and sustainable community development through evidence-based advocacy, continuous learning, and purposeful service.
